Security reviews stall migrations. Not because Jira Cloud can’t pass them – it can – but because enterprise procurement teams ask questions that no one on the migration project has thought through, and suddenly a go-live date turns into a two-month standstill while everyone scrambles for answers. These questions aren’t hypothetical.
